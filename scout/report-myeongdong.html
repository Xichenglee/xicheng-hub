A first-timer's eating walk through Myeongdong

6 stops ~20 min on foot About ₩13,000 a meal

You are in Myeongdong for the first time and you want real local food, about $10 a meal. Here is what I would actually do: six stops, one full day of eating, all within a short walk of the station.

Brief: first visit to Seoul, staying around Myeongdong, eats anything, authentic local preferred, roughly ₩13,000 a meal. Below: all six picks, with hours checked on each listing.

✓ Checked at street level ✓ Hours verified on each listing ✓ All walkable from the station ✓ One honest swap

Shinsun Seolnongtang

Myeong-dong 2-ga 2-2, 2F · Jung District
Order this Seolleongtang (ox-bone soup) about ₩11,000

The white, milky soup Koreans eat for breakfast. It arrives plain on purpose — you salt it at the table until it tastes right, then tip the rice in.

Also try Garlic seolleongtang

Same soup with a heavier garlic hit. Kimchi is self-serve and unlimited.

What people say

“The Seolleongtang (ox bone soup) had a very mild flavor and they recommended seasoning it to our own preference with salt, pepper. It came with a portion of rice meant to be added into the soup.”

Wanderlog

Reviewers note a clean, spacious room, all-you-can-eat kimchi, and easy seating for solo diners.

Google Maps
Why this one
Open at 8am, which is rare in Myeongdong. This is the local breakfast, not a tourist one.
Getting there
Second floor on the Myeongdong 2-ga lane, just off the main shopping street. Look up for the sign.
Hours
Daily 8am–9pm

Myeongdong Kyoja

129 Toegye-ro · Jung District
Order this Kalguksu (knife-cut noodles) about ₩11,000

Hand-cut noodles in a chicken broth boiled down for hours. The dumplings already come in the soup, so you do not need to order them separately.

Also try Mandu (pork dumplings)

If you want them anyway, they are the other half of the order. Two to a plate, bigger than they look.

What people say

“The noodles come with dumplings in the soup, so you don’t need to order the dumplings. If I had to choose only 1, I’d go for the knife-cut handmade kal-guksu noodles.”

A Love Letter to Asia

“They don’t allow ordering just one bowl of kalguksu to share. Sharing kalguksu plus dumplings between two people is acceptable.”

Lemon8
Why this one
The most famous bowl in Myeongdong, and it holds up. Four things on the menu. Pay before you eat; the line moves fast.
Getting there
On Toegye-ro, a short walk from Myeongdong Station. Shared tables and a queue at peak — go before noon.
Hours
Daily 10:30am–9pm

Ssada Gimbap · Euljiro Entrance

44 Myeongdong 3-gil, B1 · Jung District
Order this Gimbap (seaweed rice roll) under ₩6,000

Everyday Korean food, made fresh and cut to order. Order at the kiosk, eat in ten minutes.

Also try Kimchi stew or pork-cutlet gimbap

If you want a hot bowl instead of a roll. Both are under ₩8,000.

What people say

“Highly recommended by both locals and tourists for its great food, cleanliness, quick service, and affordable prices.”

Wanderlog

“It was surprisingly good! Super cheap as well.”

Reddit, r/koreatravel
Why this one
The cheapest real meal on this list, and it is open around the clock. The move when every other queue is long.
Getting there
Basement level on Myeongdong 3-gil, by the Euljiro entrance. Take the stairs down and order at the kiosk.
Hours
24 hours, daily

Gamekol Son Wangmandu

42 Namdaemunsijang 4-gil · Namdaemun Market
Order this Son wangmandu (king dumplings) ₩8,000–9,000 with kalguksu

Fist-sized handmade dumplings, steamed, with a pork filling that actually tastes of pork. Get a set at the counter and eat standing in the market.

Also try Spicy kalguksu

The other half of what this stall is known for. Ask for the noodles with the dumplings.

What people say

“Great dumplings and knife noodles. Handmade noodles, giant dumplings, and flavorful pork filling.”

Postcard, @davidcho

“If you go to Namdaemun Market and don’t stop by here, you’re missing out. The takeout line was ridiculously long.”

Trip.com
Why this one
A 40-year-old market stall, ten minutes south of Myeongdong. This is where the locals queue for dumplings, not for a photo.
Getting there
Inside Namdaemun Market, in the food alley. From Myeongdong Station walk south on Namdaemun-ro, about 10 minutes.
Hours
Mon–Sat 8:20am–7:30pm · closed Sunday

Bbandak Bbandak Myeongdong

46 Myeongdong 4-gil, 2F · Jung District
Order this Dakgalbi (spicy stir-fried chicken) about ₩14,000 a person

Chicken, cabbage and rice cake cooked on a hot plate at your table. The rose-cream version is the one people come for.

Also try Add ramen noodles or cheese

Dropped into the pan at the end. This is what the regulars do.

What people say

“Fresh ingredients, generous portions, and unforgettable cheesy flavors.”

Tripadvisor

“Service is fast, portions are large, and prices are reasonable.”

Trip.com
Why this one
A table-cooking meal, so it is better with at least one other person. A full order runs a little over $10; two people sharing one pan lands under it.
Getting there
Second floor on Myeongdong 4-gil, a side lane off the main street. Look for the yellow sign.
Hours
Daily 11am–10:30pm

Myth Jokbal Myeongdong

21 Myeongdong 3-gil · Jung District
Order this Jokbal (braised pig trotters) about ₩15,000 a person, shared

Sliced, glossy, braised until it gives way. Wrapped in lettuce with raw garlic and ssamjang. One plate feeds two or three.

Also try Half-and-half (jokbal + bossam)

Half pig trotter, half boiled pork belly. The way to try both without over-ordering.

What people say

“The jokbal itself had no gamey smell and a chewy texture — especially good. The restaurant says they boil it more than six times a day.”

Superchart

“The meat is very soft and tender, and it is really perfect with his garlic sauce.”

Klook
Why this one
Open until 3am. If you eat one late meal in Seoul, make it this one. Order a single plate for the table.
Getting there
On Myeongdong 3-gil, a couple of minutes from the station. It fills up late; put your name down.
Hours
Daily 11am–3am

The walk

  1. Shinsun Seolnongtang8am — start with the local breakfast soup.
  2. Myeongdong Kyoja11am — the famous bowl, before the lunch queue.
  3. Ssada Gimbap2pm — a cheap roll when you need a break.
  4. Gamekol Son Wangmandu5pm — the market detour south, dumplings standing.
  5. Bbandak Bbandak7:30pm — sit down and cook dinner at the table.
  6. Myth Jokbal10pm — one shared plate before it closes at 3am.

About 20 minutes of walking between stops, plus 10 minutes each way to Namdaemun Market. Eat as you go.

The one swap: the skewer stalls along the main Myeongdong shopping street look like the local food. They are mostly priced for tourists and you eat standing. Every stop above is a real restaurant, and most of them cost less.
