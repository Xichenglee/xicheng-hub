New Po Heng Lumpia House

621 Carvajal St, Binondo
Order this Lumpiang Sariwa (fresh lumpia)

The Hokkien-style wrap, rolled to order. One order is big enough that a single roll satisfies most people.

Also try Pork Asado Siopao or Ube Pao

Steamed, less than a dollar each. Reviewers call them “damn addicting.”

What people say

“Bought 1 lumpia for only 95 pesos… this is the best lumpia I’ve ever tasted. Superb. It’s huge, filling, serving is very generous.”

Tripadvisor

“They serve the best fresh lumpiang sariwa in town. The Hokkien-style wrap is the size of a burrito.”

The Infatuation
Why this one
The fresh-lumpia benchmark in Binondo. The line at lunch is locals, not tours, and that is the whole review.
Getting there
Carvajal St runs parallel to Ongpin, one block south. Look for the sign, not the queue.
Hours
Mon–Sat 8:30am–7pm · Sun 8:30am–6pm

Sincerity Restaurant

519 Quintin Paredes, Binondo
Order this Sincerity Fried Chicken

The dish Metro Manila food writers keep coming back for. About ₱160 for a half order.

Also try The fresh lumpia and Shanghai lumpia

The sit-down counterpart to the two counter stops. Fried, crisp, sweet dipping sauce.

What people say

“Must orders would have to be: tao pao… one of the best in Binondo. Price, service and ambiance is perfect for the area.”

Yelp

“Their chicken is tastier and not boring, the vegetables are crispier, and it’s really tastier in Binondo.”

Reddit, r/PHFoodPorn
Why this one
The old-school sit-down house. Do this one properly, at a table, once in the day.
Getting there
Quintin Paredes, in the Binondo core. Walkable from Ongpin in a few minutes.
Hours
Mon–Thu 8:30am–7pm · Fri–Sat 8:30am–8pm · Sun 8:30am–2pm

Eng Bee Tin

628 Ongpin St, Binondo
Order this Ube Hopia

The purple-yam pastry that pulled the shop back from the edge. Dense, not too sweet.

Also try Mochipia or Hopia Combi

Mochipia is hopia with a mochi center; Hopia Combi is two flavors in one.

What people say

“Your go-to place for that good ube or munggo hopia. The staff are always very helpful too.”

Tripadvisor

Credited the ube hopia with saving the shop — it is now the flavor everyone buys.

Philippine Daily Inquirer
Why this one
The sweet finish. You have eaten three savoury things; this is what you carry home. On Ongpin since 1912.
Getting there
On Ongpin itself. Buy the hopia at the counter and eat it walking.
Hours
Daily 7am–10pm

The walk

  1. New Po Heng — Carvajal StStart here, eat standing.
  2. Sincerity — Quintin ParedesSit down and do it properly.
  3. Eng Bee Tin — Ongpin StBuy hopia for the walk home.

About 15 minutes of walking total. Eat as you go.

The one swap: everyone sends visitors to the Instagram-famous place on Ongpin. Skip that line — the two houses above do the same thing better, and neither is a photo stop.
