Quick questions

Will the character look the same on all three sheets?

That is the whole point. I check pose by pose against your reference so the face, hair and outfit hold from sheet to sheet.

Can I mix outfits across the sheets?

Yes, put it in the brief. Otherwise I keep one outfit so the set reads as one character, not three.

How do I receive the pack?

Transparent PNGs for all eighteen poses, print-friendly sizes, by email or one link within 3 days.

What if a sheet drifts?

One revision per sheet. Flag the sheet and the part that drifted, and I redo it.
